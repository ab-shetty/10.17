# 2024 events that plausibly affected San Pedro Bay anchorage queues (POLA / POLB)

Compiled from web searches and page fetches. **18 rows, not the 20–40 requested.** I kept only events I could source. I found nothing citable for 2024 fog or wind pilot-station closures, terminal outages, or Lunar New Year blank-sailing counts, so those are omitted rather than guessed.

Caveat for the forecast: volumes were very high in 2024, but POLA's own Oct 2024 release says cargo moved "without a single ship backed up at sea". Volume surges did not translate into big anchorage queues, which supports Risk 1 in CLAUDE.md. Treat "up" below as upward pressure on the queue, not a confirmed queue spike.

Verified = I opened the page. Secondary = the figure comes from a search snippet or a news reprint.

## Months POLA / POLB reported record or near-record TEUs in 2024

**POLB**
- Aug 2024: 913,873 TEU. All-time monthly record at the time.
- Oct 2024: 987,191 TEU. New all-time record.
- Nov 2024: 884,154 TEU. Best November on record.
- Dec 2024: 861,006 TEU. gCaptain labels this a monthly record; I read it as a best-December claim but did not confirm the wording.
- Q4 2024: busiest quarter ever. Full year 9,649,724 TEU, an annual record.
- Not found: Jan–Jul and Sep monthly figures.

**POLA**
- Jan 2024: 855,652 TEU, near-record (1.1% under Jan 2022). Secondary.
- Jul 2024: 939,600 TEU. Best July ever and busiest month in over two years.
- Aug 2024: 960,597 TEU. Near-record and busiest non-pandemic month.
- Sep 2024: 954,706 TEU. Q3 was the busiest quarter in port history. Secondary.
- Oct 2024: 905,026 TEU. Record October and the fourth straight month above 900k. Verified.
- Not records: Feb (781,434), Apr (770,337), May (752,893), Jun (827,757) and Nov (884,315, +16%, no record claimed).
- Not found: Mar and Dec 2024, and the full-year total.

## Gaps and caveats
- I found no 2024 sources for pilot-station fog or wind closures, terminal outages, or West Coast blank-sailing counts.
- No source quantifies a 2024 San Pedro Bay anchorage queue. The Marine Exchange of Southern California is the likely source. The 2024 AIS data in `data/ais/` is the ground truth for the queue.
- The Marine Exchange and the ports do not appear to have published vessel-queue counts for 2024 in anything I found.
- POLA and POLB figures differ slightly between the ports' releases and press reprints. Prefer the portoflosangeles.org and polb.com pages where both exist.
